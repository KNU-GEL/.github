# Global Ecology Lab (GEL)

**Kangwon National University · Chuncheon, Republic of Korea**

The Global Ecology Lab (GEL) studies environmental and ecosystem dynamics using Earth observation, field observations, geospatial analysis, and data-driven modeling.

## Research

Our research explores environmental change and ecosystem processes across spatial and temporal scales, with an emphasis on connecting observations and methods across scales.

## Research Code

This organization hosts research software, analysis code, and reproducible workflows developed through ongoing and published research at the Global Ecology Lab.
